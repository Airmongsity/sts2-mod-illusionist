# Illusionist v0.5.4

本日志描述相对已发布 `v0.5.3` 的最终净变化。发布检查器报告：新增 0 项、移除 0 项、6 张牌发生净变化；下列跨卡牌与兼容性修复由人工复核补充。

## 平衡性

- 【虚晃】未升级时添加消耗；升级改为移除消耗，不再降低费用。
- 新增【严寒】：【战斗开始时，冻结这张牌】。【逆转】、【抢拍】与【催化】获得严寒，侧边栏同时显示冻结的效果说明。
- 【导能】获得的能量由 X-1 调整为 X/2（向下取整）。

## 问题修复

- 修复【客串】与【大假面舞会】直接把规范卡牌模板用于角色选择，导致选牌界面失败、【客串】无法完成幻化的问题。
- 修复镜像等自动打牌流程中的【突袭】击杀最后一名敌人后，仍尝试注册已无意义的幻化回退而导致战斗卡死的问题。
- 为 `CuteAncientIllustrationFZ-Cards 1.0.0` 缓存并重用已卸载卡图纹理的问题添加窄范围兼容保护；其他异常仍会正常抛出并保留诊断信息。

## Balance

- Feint now Exhausts before upgrade. Its upgrade removes Exhaust instead of reducing its Energy cost.
- Added Severe Cold: a card with it starts combat Frozen. Reversal, Cut In, and Catalyze now have Severe Cold, and their sidebars also explain Frozen.
- Channel now refunds X/2 Energy, rounded down, instead of X-1.

## Fixes

- Fixed Cameo and Grand Masquerade passing canonical card templates directly to character selection, which broke the selection screen and prevented Cameo from completing its Transmute.
- Fixed combat freezing when an auto-played Ambush killed the final enemy and still attempted to register a now-irrelevant Transmute revert.
- Added a narrowly scoped compatibility guard for `CuteAncientIllustrationFZ-Cards 1.0.0` caching and reusing an unloaded card texture; unrelated exceptions still propagate for diagnosis.
