# Illusionist v0.6.0

相对已发布 v0.5.8 的最终变化 / Final changes since published v0.5.8.

## 兼容性与视觉更新

- 适配 Slay the Spire II 0.113.0，需要 RitsuLib 0.6.12 或更新版本。
- 更新角色战斗、休息处、事件、多人手势，以及卡牌、能力、遗物、药水和界面美术。角色选择页保留原有美术。
- 战斗鬼火改用新的粒子渲染：火焰自然上升、漂浮并随风偏移；获得格挡时逐层积累残影，打出能力牌时加速并展开轨道，攻击命中时产生火花。
- 调整镜像之间的前后遮挡，更新坐姿休息动画和假商人事件姿态。
- 为易容、灼热接入专属卡牌覆盖效果，并更新严寒覆盖美术。

## 卡牌调整

- **嬗变**：耗能 2 → 1；基础伤害 12 → 8，每次本回合变化的额外伤害 7 → 5。升级改为基础伤害 11、每次变化额外伤害 6。
- **破幕**：耗能 2 → 1；打出时抽 1 张牌。破盾收益改为每回合首次突破敌人格挡时，抽 1 张牌并获得 1 点能量。
- **清算**：耗能 2 → 1，新增消耗。伤害调整为 12（升级后 16），本场战斗每打出一张描述含有“意图”的牌，伤害增加 8（升级后 10）。
- **拆穿**：耗能 2 → 1；基础伤害 13 → 9，仍按目标当前格挡增加伤害。升级改为基础伤害 13。
- **积蓄**：新增消耗，基础伤害 11 → 18。成长改为本场战斗中所有敌人累计每获得 2 点格挡，伤害增加 1 点；升级改为基础伤害 24，不再降低耗能。

## 修复

- 修复幻化后的新牌被其他 MOD 自动移出手牌时，旧牌外观仍残留的问题，包含大假面舞会的相关交互。
- 适配新版死亡回调及幻形使用的随机洗牌接口，保留既有卡牌效果和随机流。

## Compatibility and Visual Update

- Updated for Slay the Spire II 0.113.0. Requires RitsuLib 0.6.12 or later.
- Refreshed combat, campsite, event, multiplayer gesture, card, power, relic, potion, and UI artwork. Character-selection artwork is preserved.
- Added the new native particle-fire renderer: rising, drifting flames; gradually accumulating guard afterimages; faster, separated orbits after Power cards; and sparks on attack contact.
- Improved depth ordering between mirror clones, added the seated campsite animation, and updated the Fake Merchant event pose.
- Added dedicated Disguised and Scorching card overlays and refreshed the Frozen overlay artwork.

## Card Changes

- **Metamorphosis**: cost 2 → 1; base damage 12 → 8; bonus per transformation this turn 7 → 5. Its upgrade now raises base damage to 11 and the transformation bonus to 6.
- **Curtain Break**: cost 2 → 1; draws 1 card when played. Its Block-break reward now triggers only the first time each turn, drawing 1 card and granting 1 Energy.
- **Reckoning**: cost 2 → 1; now Exhausts. Deals 12 damage (16 upgraded), plus 8 (10 upgraded) per card mentioning an enemy's intent played this combat.
- **Call the Bluff**: cost 2 → 1; base damage 13 → 9, still increased by the target's current Block. Its upgrade now raises base damage to 13.
- **Accrue**: now Exhausts; base damage 11 → 18. Gains 1 damage for every 2 Block gained by all enemies across this combat. Its upgrade now raises base damage to 24 instead of reducing its cost.

## Fixes

- Fixed stale hand-card visuals when another MOD automatically moves a newly transmuted card out of the hand, including related Grand Masquerade interactions.
- Updated death hooks and Shapeshift's shuffle integration for the new game API while preserving existing effects and RNG stream usage.
